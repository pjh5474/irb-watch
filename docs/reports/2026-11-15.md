- **Where sessions come from (CAL-O1):** manual entry plus AI proposals now. A platform admin starts "Find sessions" per hospital; the job writes proposals only.
- **Who enters them (CAL-O2):** platform admins enter and review; users who work on a project with that hospital propose.

- **Reminders (CAL-O3):** reuse A2's days and recipients; proposals remind too, marked.

- **Step link:** the user picks; a hint is shown.

- **Tasks:** simple fields; edit access changes them.
- **AI search:** runs on demand as an admin job and reads only boards with AI on.

## Defaults I chose while building (please check)
- **The AI's quote and its stored post are shown to platform admins only.** The post may be on a members-only board, and every user reads sessions.
- **A task whose assignee may no longer work on the project** reminds the owner and PM instead. The title belongs to the project.
- **A passed cutoff is not repeated** in the mail. A step's due date still repeats weekly, as in A2.
- **The AI search shares the 2-thread job pool with drafts.** One open search per hospital, 2 per admin.
- **The views are built by hand.** No calendar library and no new dependency. The owner's option had mentioned "likely a library".
- **Weeks start on Monday.**

## Done
**Backend**
- Migration file: tables `session_kinds` (seeded 정기심의, 신속심의, 기타), `irb_sessions`, `session_jobs`, `project_tasks`, and column `pipeline_steps.target_session_id`.
  - All tables are server-only: revoke first, then grant.
  - One live, non-rejected session per hospital, kind and day.
  - A trigger keeps a task's site and step inside its project and copies the project's organization.
- **Sessions** (`services/sessions.py`):
  - kinds; admin create, edit, state moves, remove; user proposals and withdraw;
  - the hint for a step (the next approved session with an open cutoff, else a proposal);
  - the AI job: schedule titles first, at most 15 posts, 8,000 characters each, 60,000 in total, `store=False`, closing tags defused;
  - every answer field is checked again: dates, cutoff not after the meeting, kind, post number;
  - duplicates and days an admin already rejected are skipped;
  - usage is counted in the audit log (`session.find_started` / `_finished`);
  - open jobs are failed at start; finished jobs are purged after 30 days.
- **Tasks** (`services/tasks.py`): the project access rule; the log keeps ids and field names only; removed tasks are purged after 30 days.
- **Calendar** (`services/calendar.py`, `GET /calendar`):
  - step dates, sessions (meeting and cutoff) and tasks over the projects the user works on;
  - at most 62 days;
  - also returns the maps the filters need.
- **Step page:** target meeting, upcoming sessions, the hint, and a warning when the target was cancelled, rejected or removed. A failed session read shows "unavailable" instead of failing the page.
- **Mail:** cutoff lines (marked "확정 전" for proposals) and task lines join the step reminders, under the same switch.

**Frontend**
- `/calendar` with month, week and list views. Filters: project, site, hospital, kind, type, hide done. A session panel shows its facts, the user's steps aiming for it, and "withdraw" for one's own proposal. A "propose a session" form.
- The step page's "Target IRB meeting" section.
- The project page's Tasks section.
- The admin tab "IRB sessions": review proposals, sessions per hospital, Find with AI with job status, the kinds editor.
- Nav item "Calendar"; ko and en texts.
