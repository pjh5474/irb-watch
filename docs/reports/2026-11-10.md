## Problems found and fixed (mostly mine)
- **Code reviews:** three `/code-review` rounds on R-BAY, logins and reviews found real bugs, all fixed:
  - a refused 입장하기 at one hospital blocked the others
  - timeouts counted as wrong passwords
  - a manual scan couldn't lift a pause
  - a session could be logged out while in use
  - the upload happened before the content check
  - the zip size limit was too loose
  - an audit entry could record an OK that didn't happen
  - the approval wasn't tied to the file
  - the admin ID was readable by users
- **I chose the wrong r-bay login model at first** (one login for all hospitals); you corrected it to one per hospital.

- **R-BAY download keys:** my check accepted only hex keys and refused older posts' base64 keys.
- **Doubled text in messages:** the post number in scan errors and the file name in zip warnings were each shown twice.
- **Times off by 9 hours:** they showed in UTC because the frontend had no time zone set.
- **A missing login paused sources** (SNUH too); it now only flags them.
- **The source stayed red after an OK** until the next scan; fixed and waiting for your commit.
- **Two r-bay sessions were left open** by my early probes (one logout timed out, one direct `/logout` was refused); they ended on R-BAY's idle timeout.
